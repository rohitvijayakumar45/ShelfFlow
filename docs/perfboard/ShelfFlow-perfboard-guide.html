ShelfFlow perfboard build guide

Shelf controller (ESP32 #1), three HX711 load-cell amplifiers, two RC522 readers, LCD and buttons, ESP32-CAM and its programmer (ESP32 #2). Board 55 × 37 pads, 2.54 mm pitch. Pins match the running firmware (firmware/shelf-controller, build devkitc-v4-usb-integration).

How the layout saves work. Pins that share a connection are placed next to each other, so one straight bare wire (a bus) or a solder bridge joins them:
  • Three rails in neighbouring rows (R1 5V, R2 GND, R3 3V3). Capacitors sit straight across two rails: no wires.
  • HX711s side by side with their headers on the same rows: one bus powers all three GND pins (R8), one all three VCC pins (R11).
  • RC522 headers back to back (C20 | C21): the six shared pins are joined by solder bridges.
  • One 6-pin UI header for the LCD and both buttons, with a single GND pin.
  • ESPs are not on the board. Female dupont ends go on the ESP pins; the other end is soldered to the pad shown.
  • Camera flashing never touches the board: those links run ESP to ESP, only while flashing.

Check your HX711 header order. This layout assumes the silkscreen reads GND, DT, SCK, VCC from top to bottom. If yours reads differently (for example VCC, SCK, DT, GND), rotate all three modules the same way so GND lands on R8 and VCC on R11, or move the two buses to the rows where GND and VCC actually are. The DT and SCK pads in the tables move with them.
RC522 cable order. Both headers are pin 1 (SDA) at the top (R22) and 3V3 at the bottom (R29), the same order as the pins on the reader module. Plug each reader cable so that the reader's SDA wire lands on R22.

6. Before you start (30 min)

  1. Baseline on the breadboard. Stop the USB bridge, then run .\.venv\Scripts\pio.exe device monitor -p COM3 -b 115200 --dtr 0 --rts 0. Save 10 DIAG raw … lines; expect rc522=0xB2/0xB2 and lcd=ok. Type calstatus and note the version.
  2. Photograph the breadboard, especially the load-cell wires on each HX711.
  3. Label with tape: load-cell cables A/B/C, HX711s A/B/C, readers R1 (left of the tray front) and R2 (right).
  4. Print page 1 of the schematic at 100 % and check one 10-pad span measures 25.4 mm.
  5. Power off: USB and the 5 V supply unplugged.
  6. Kit: iron at 320–350 °C with a fine tip, 0.6–0.8 mm rosin-core solder, flux, multimeter with beep, cutters, stripper, tinned bare wire (rails and buses), insulated solid-core 22–24 AWG, an 8-pin male header ×2, a 6-pin male header, a 2-pin screw terminal, 100 µF ×2, 10 µF ×1, about 25 female-ended dupont wires, tape or cable ties.

7. Build order

Soldering rules.
  • Heat pad and pin together for 2–3 s, then feed solder. You want a shiny cone.
  • Modules and headers: solder one corner pin, check the part sits flat, then the rest.
  • Bus: lay bare wire flat on the solder side along the row, and solder it to every pin pad on the row plus every 3–4 empty pads.
  • Bridge: tin both pads, then add solder until the blobs merge (or lay a cut-off lead across both).
  • After every joint, look for an unwanted bridge to the next pad. Clear it with wick, or by dragging a clean tip between the pads.

8. Inspection before power

  • Joints are shiny cones; there are no balls or gaps.
  • There are no bridges except the R1↔R2 ones and C3's two. Look at the board against a light.
  • No wire clippings are on the board (shake it upside down).
  • Capacitor stripes (−) are on GND.

9. Power-up and tests

  1. 5 V supply only, no ESPs plugged. 5V rail ≈ 5.0 V; 3V3 rail ≈ 0 V (ESP #1 feeds it). Nothing gets warm. Disconnect.
  2. Plug ESP #1, USB only. The 3V3 rail reads ≈ 3.3 V. Open the serial monitor (command in section 6).
    • The LCD shows ShelfFlow sensor, then readings.
    • You see DIAG raw A=… B=… C=… … rc522=0xB2/0xB2 … lcd=ok, with no DISCONNECTED_, STUCK_ or SATURATED_ flags.
    • Press over each cell: only that cell's raw value jumps.
    • Tap a tag on R1, then R2: the scans= counters rise. The r1, r2 and both commands test each reader alone.
    • PAGE changes the LCD page; ACTION toggles the backlight.
  3. Add the 5 V supply (USB stays). Same results.
  4. Plug the ESP-CAM (IO0 jumper off). It joins "Shaun" within about 60 s. The DIAG values must not jump when its Wi-Fi starts.
  5. Start the services (docs/local-hardware-integration.md). In the staff app, Device health shows sensor-1 recently seen.

10. Recalibration

The cells were re-soldered, so recalibrate:

  1. Leave everything powered for 20 minutes to warm up.
  2. Hold PAGE + ACTION for 3 s, then follow the LCD: empty, weight over A, B and C, verify, hold ACTION to save. The known mass is 44 g (set another with mass <g>).
  3. Empty tray reads ≈ 0 g; the weight reads within ±2 g in the centre and at each corner, and holds for a minute.
  4. Measure the support positions in mm and set the tray geometry in the staff app.
